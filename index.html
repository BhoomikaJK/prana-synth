PRANA

The Kinetic Synthesizer

How to Play

  • The Keys: Rest your left hand on the middle row (A to K) for white keys, and the top row (W to U) for black keys.
  • The Bellows/Bow: Swipe your right hand across your trackpad (or move your mouse).
  • The Rule: The instrument only breathes when you move. If you stop, the sound fades.
